📘 A School Project

1. Reading Text

Last month, our English teacher gave us a school project about modern technology.

We had to work in small groups and prepare a presentation.

My group chose the topic of artificial intelligence in everyday life.

At first, we didn't know where to start, so we decided to meet after school.

We searched the Internet for information and found some interesting examples.

I can use computers quite well, so I was responsible for finding information and preparing the slides.

Anna is good at speaking English, so she could present our project to the class.

However, we soon realized that we had too much information.

Our teacher told us that we should choose only the most important ideas.

We also had to check all the information carefully because some websites were unreliable.

Yesterday, we finished the presentation and practised it several times.

I was nervous at first, but my friends said that I could do it.

Tomorrow, we will present our project in front of the whole class.

We must speak clearly and not read everything from the screen.

Our teacher may ask us some difficult questions after the presentation.

If that happens, we should stay calm and try to answer together.

We are a little worried, but we think our project will be interesting.

After the presentation, shall we celebrate with some pizza?

4. Past Simple

В тексте Past Simple используется для действий, которые уже произошли.

Примеры:

Our teacher gave us a project.
We chose the topic.
We searched the Internet.
We found some examples.
We finished the presentation.

Обрати внимание на неправильные глаголы:

Present Past Перевод
give gave дать
choose chose выбрать
find found найти
have had иметь
say said сказать

5. Present Simple

Present Simple используется для фактов, привычек и постоянных характеристик.

I can use computers quite well.
Anna is good at speaking English.
Some websites are unreliable.
We are a little worried.

В третьем лице единственного числа обычно появляется окончание -s:

He works.
She speaks.
It looks.

6. Future Simple

Future Simple используется, когда действие произойдёт в будущем.

Tomorrow, we will present our project.
Our teacher may ask some questions.
We think our project will be interesting.

Формула:

Subject + will + verb

Важно: после will используется обычная форма глагола.

I will go. ✅
I will goes. ❌
I will went. ❌

7. Modal Verbs

Глагол Значение Пример
can мочь, уметь I can use computers.
could мог, умел She could present the project.
must должен We must speak clearly.
should следует, стоит We should stay calm.
may может быть, возможно She may ask a question.
shall давай / предлагаю Shall we celebrate?
had to пришлось, был вынужден We had to check the information.
Важно: после can, could, must, should и may используется обычная форма глагола.

She can speak English. ✅
She can speaks English. ❌

We should stay calm. ✅
We should stays calm. ❌
