🧠 Present Continuous Exercises

1. Утвердительная форма

Раскрой скобки.

  • I ___ (read) a book now.
  • She ___ (cook) dinner.
  • They ___ (play) football.
  • We ___ (study) English.
  • He ___ (watch) TV.
  • The baby ___ (sleep).
  • You ___ (write) an email.
  • My parents ___ (work).
  • The dog ___ (run).
  • It ___ (rain).

2. Отрицательная форма

Сделай отрицание.

  • I am reading. → ______
  • She is cooking. → ______
  • They are playing football. → ______
  • We are studying. → ______
  • He is sleeping. → ______
  • The baby is crying. → ______
  • You are listening. → ______
  • My father is driving. → ______
  • It is snowing. → ______
  • We are waiting. → ______

4. Вставь am / is / are

  • I ___ eating.
  • She ___ watching TV.
  • They ___ running.
  • We ___ learning English.
  • He ___ sleeping.
  • You ___ listening.
  • The cat ___ drinking milk.
  • My friends ___ waiting.
  • It ___ snowing.
  • The children ___ playing.

  • play → ______

  • swim → ______
  • sit → ______
  • dance → ______
  • make → ______
  • write → ______

6. Выбери правильный вариант

  • I (read / am reading) now.
  • Look! It (rains / is raining).
  • She (cooks / is cooking) dinner at the moment.
  • We (study / are studying) this week.
  • Listen! Someone (sings / is singing).
  • They (play / are playing) football now.
  • He (drives / is driving) to work now.
  • The baby (cries / is crying).
  • My sister (does homework / is doing homework).
  • You (watch / are watching) TV right now.

  • He am sleeping.
  • We are cook dinner.
  • It are raining.
  • You is listening.
  • The cat am eating.

  • He is makeing tea.

10. Present Simple или Present Continuous?

Выберите правильную форму.

  • I usually ______ to work by bus. (go / am going)
  • Look! The dog ______. (runs / is running)
  • She always ______ coffee in the morning. (drinks / is drinking)
  • We ______ TV now. (watch / are watching)
  • He ______ English every day. (studies / is studying)
  • Listen! Somebody ______. (sings / is singing)
  • My father ______ in a bank. (works / is working)
  • They ______ tennis every Sunday. (play / are playing)
  • I ______ my homework at the moment. (do / am doing)
  • She ______ dinner now. (cooks / is cooking)

🔥 Сложные упражнения (Intermediate)

1. Переведи на английский

  • Почему ты сейчас улыбаешься?
  • Мы сегодня работаем дома.
  • Они сейчас не смотрят телевизор.
  • Куда вы идете?
  • Он разговаривает со своим начальником.

2. Раскрой скобки

  • She ___ (not / sleep).
  • They ___ (play) basketball now.
  • ___ he ___ (work) today?
  • We ___ (study) for the exam.
  • I ___ (not / watch) TV.

3. Исправь ошибки

  • She are cooking now.
  • I am study English.
  • They is waiting.
  • He are writing a letter.
  • We am listening to music.
