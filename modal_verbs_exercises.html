🧠 Modal Verbs Exercises

1. Выбери правильный модальный глагол

Выбери: can, must, should, may или shall.

  • You ___ wear a seat belt in a car.
  • ___ I open the window?
  • She ___ speak English and Spanish.
  • You look tired. You ___ go to bed earlier.
  • It ___ rain later, so take an umbrella.
  • ___ we order some food?
  • Students ___ be quiet during the exam.
  • He ___ help you with the computer. He knows a lot about it.
  • You ___ see a doctor if the pain continues.
  • ___ I carry your bag?

2. Отрицательная форма

3. Составь вопросы

Поставь слова в правильном порядке.

  • you / can / swim → ____________________?
  • I / may / come in → ____________________?
  • we / should / wait → ____________________?
  • she / can / drive → ____________________?
  • I / should / call him → ____________________?

4. Смешанные упражнения

Выбери правильный вариант.

  • She (can / cans) speak English.
  • You (must / must to) finish this today.
  • He (should / shoulds) see a doctor.
  • (May / Do may) I come in?
  • We (can / can to) help you.
  • You (mustn't / don't must) touch this.
  • She (can't / doesn't can) drive.
  • (Should / Do should) I call him?
  • (Shall / Do shall) we go for a walk?
  • He (may / mays) be late.

5. Исправь ошибки

В каждом предложении есть ошибка.

  • She can to swim very well.
  • You must to finish your homework.
  • He cans speak French.
  • She don't can drive.
  • Should I to call him?
  • May I to use your phone?
  • He shoulds study more.
  • We mustn't to be late.
  • Do you can help me?
  • Shall we to go home?

6. Mustn't или don't have to?

Выбери правильный вариант.

  • You ___ wear a uniform. It's not necessary. (mustn't / don't have to)
  • You ___ smoke here. It's forbidden. (mustn't / don't have to)
  • You ___ bring any food. We already have enough. (mustn't / don't have to)
  • You ___ tell anyone. It's a secret. (mustn't / don't have to)
  • She ___ come to the meeting if she is busy. (mustn't / doesn't have to)
  • Visitors ___ touch the paintings. (mustn't / don't have to)
  • We ___ hurry. We have plenty of time. (mustn't / don't have to)
  • Children ___ play with this machine. (mustn't / don't have to)

8. Выбери модальный глагол по ситуации

Прочитай ситуацию и выбери подходящий вариант.

  • You are very tired. You ___ go to bed. (can / should / may)
  • There is a sign: "No Parking". You ___ park here. (can / mustn't / should)
  • You know how to speak French. You ___ speak French. (can / must / may)
  • Your teacher says the exam is tomorrow. You ___ study tonight. (must / can / may)
  • You are asking a teacher for permission to leave. "___ I leave early?" (Can / Must / Shall)
  • You are not sure about tomorrow's weather. "It ___ rain." (must / may / can)
  • You want to suggest going to a restaurant. "___ we go to a restaurant?" (Must / Shall / May)
  • Your friend has a headache. You give advice: "You ___ take a rest." (should / can / mustn't)

13. Выбери правильный смысл

  • You mustn't leave.
    а) Тебе необязательно уходить.
    б) Тебе нельзя уходить.

  • You don't have to leave.
    а) Тебе необязательно уходить.
    б) Тебе нельзя уходить.

  • You should leave.
    а) Тебе следует уйти.
    б) Ты обязан уйти.

  • You can leave.
    а) Ты можешь уйти.
    б) Ты должен уйти.

  • You may leave.
    а) Тебе можно уйти.
    б) Тебе обязательно нужно уйти.

🚀 15. Challenge — выбери правильное значение

Обрати внимание на смысл предложения.

  • She may be at home.
    а) Возможно, она дома.
    б) Она обязана быть дома.

  • You should be more careful.
    а) Тебе следует быть осторожнее.
    б) Ты умеешь быть осторожнее.
