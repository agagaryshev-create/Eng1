🧠 Future Simple Exercises

1. Утвердительная форма

Раскрой скобки.

  • I ___ (call) you tomorrow.
  • She ___ (visit) her grandmother next week.
  • They ___ (play) football tomorrow.
  • We ___ (study) English tonight.
  • He ___ (watch) TV later.
  • The baby ___ (sleep) soon.
  • You ___ (write) an email tomorrow.
  • My parents ___ (work) tomorrow.
  • The dog ___ (run) in the park.
  • It ___ (rain) tomorrow.

4. Вставь will или won't

  • I ___ call you tomorrow.
  • She ___ come to school tomorrow. She is sick.
  • They ___ play football next Sunday.
  • We ___ forget your birthday.
  • He ___ help you with your homework.
  • It ___ rain tomorrow. The weather forecast says so.
  • You ___ need a coat. It will be warm.
  • My parents ___ travel next month.
  • I ___ be late.
  • She ___ buy a new phone this month.

5. Выбери правильный вариант

  • I (will call / called) you tomorrow.
  • She (will visit / visited) London next year.
  • They (will play / played) football tomorrow.
  • We (will watch / watched) a movie tonight.
  • He (will buy / bought) a new phone next month.
  • I (will see / saw) my friend later.
  • She (will go / went) to school tomorrow.
  • They (will come / came) home soon.
  • We (will have / had) dinner at 8 o'clock.
  • He (will start / started) work next Monday.

6. Исправь ошибки

  • I will went to the cinema tomorrow.
  • She will goes to school.
  • They won't played football.
  • He will saw a good movie.
  • We will had dinner at 8.
  • She won't comes tomorrow.
  • Will you watched the movie?
  • Will he goes to London?
  • I will bought a new book.
  • They will went home later.

7. Составь предложение

Пример: tomorrow / I / a movie / will watch → I will watch a movie tomorrow.

  • tomorrow / she / to school / will go
  • next week / they / football / will play
  • tonight / we / a movie / will watch
  • tomorrow / I / my friend / will see
  • next year / he / London / will visit
  • later / she / you / will call
  • soon / they / home / will come
  • next month / we / a new car / will buy
  • tomorrow morning / I / breakfast / will have
  • next Sunday / they / their grandparents / will visit

10. Решение прямо сейчас

Выбери правильный вариант.

  • The phone is ringing. I (will answer / answered) it.
  • I'm tired. I (will go / went) to bed.
  • It's cold here. I (will close / closed) the window.
  • I'm hungry. I (will make / made) a sandwich.
  • Don't worry. I (will help / helped) you.
  • This bag is heavy. I (will carry / carried) it for you.
  • It's late. I (will call / called) a taxi.
  • Someone is knocking at the door. I (will open / opened) it.
  • I'm thirsty. I (will get / got) some water.
  • You have a lot of homework. I (will help / helped) you.
