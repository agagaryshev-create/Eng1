🧠 Past Simple Exercises

1. Утвердительная форма

Раскрой скобки.

  • I ___ (watch) TV yesterday.
  • She ___ (cook) dinner last night.
  • They ___ (play) football on Sunday.
  • We ___ (study) English yesterday.
  • He ___ (visit) his friend.
  • The baby ___ (sleep) well last night.
  • You ___ (clean) your room.
  • My parents ___ (work) yesterday.
  • Tom ___ (open) the window.
  • It ___ (rain) yesterday.

6. Выбери правильный вариант

  • I (watch / watched) TV yesterday.
  • She (go / went) to school last Monday.
  • They (play / played) football yesterday.
  • We (see / saw) a good film last night.
  • He (buy / bought) a new computer.
  • My parents (visit / visited) Moscow last year.
  • I (have / had) breakfast at 8 o'clock.
  • She (come / came) home late.
  • We (take / took) a taxi.
  • He (write / wrote) an email yesterday.

7. Исправь ошибки

  • She go to school yesterday.
  • I did watched TV.
  • They didn't played football.
  • He buyed a new phone.
  • We didn't went to the cinema.
  • Did you saw him yesterday?
  • She writed an email.
  • He didn't came home.
  • They was at school yesterday.
  • I goed to the shop.

  • home / came / late / she
  • my friend / visited / I / yesterday

  • breakfast / had / I / at 8 o'clock

10. Past Simple или Present Simple?

Выберите правильную форму.

  • I usually ______ to work by bus. (go / went)
  • Yesterday I ______ to work by car. (go / went)
  • She always ______ coffee in the morning. (drinks / drank)
  • She ______ coffee yesterday morning. (drinks / drank)
  • We ______ football every Sunday. (play / played)
  • We ______ football last Sunday. (play / played)
  • He usually ______ English every day. (studies / studied)
  • He ______ English yesterday. (studies / studied)
  • I often ______ TV in the evening. (watch / watched)
  • I ______ TV last night. (watch / watched)

🔥 Сложные упражнения (Intermediate)

1. Переведи на английский

  • Что ты делал вчера вечером?
  • Почему она не пришла на работу?
  • Мы ходили в кино в прошлую субботу.
  • Они не видели этот фильм.
  • Когда ты купил эту машину?

2. Раскрой скобки

  • She ___ (not / go) to school yesterday.
  • They ___ (visit) their grandparents last weekend.
  • ___ he ___ (call) you yesterday?
  • We ___ (not / see) this film.
  • I ___ (buy) a new jacket last week.

3. Исправь ошибки

  • She didn't went to school.
  • I did watched a film.
  • They was at home yesterday.
  • He buyed a new computer.
  • Did you saw Anna yesterday?

4. Составь предложение

  • yesterday / they / to the cinema / went
  • a new car / he / bought / last week
  • did / you / where / go / yesterday?
  • we / didn't / the film / like
  • she / her grandmother / visited / last weekend
