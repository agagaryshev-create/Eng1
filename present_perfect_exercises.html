1. Раскрой скобки (Present Perfect)

  1. I __________ (finish) my homework.
  2. She __________ (buy) a new phone.
  3. They __________ (not see) this film.
  4. We __________ (visit) London three times.
  5. Tom __________ (lose) his keys.
  6. My parents __________ (arrive).
  7. Anna __________ (not call) me.
  8. The children __________ (eat) all the cake.
  9. He __________ (write) five emails today.
  10. I __________ (never / fly) by helicopter.
